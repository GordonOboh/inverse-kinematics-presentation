# Inverse Kinematics in Games

**Course:** CS650-151 HY — Artificial Intelligence  
**Instructor:** Professor Ruslan Yelizarov  
**Student:** Gordon Oboh  

A presentation exploring the role of Inverse Kinematics (IK) in creating realistic, dynamic movements within games. Topics include the technological foundations of IK from robotics, creative and commercial impacts in the gaming industry, and future directions such as predictive algorithms, XR integration, and ML-driven animation workflows.
